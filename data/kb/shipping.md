# Shipping and delivery

## Where we ship
We ship to all EU countries, the UK, Switzerland and Norway. We don't ship to PO boxes or
parcel lockers outside Germany.

## Delivery times and costs
| Destination | Standard | Express |
|---|---|---|
| Germany | 2-3 business days, free over €50 | next business day, €9.90 |
| Rest of EU | 3-6 business days, €4.90 | 1-2 business days, €14.90 |
| UK, CH, NO | 4-8 business days, €9.90 | not available |

Orders placed before 2 pm CET on a business day leave our warehouse the same day.

## Tracking
You get a tracking link by email as soon as the parcel leaves us. Tracking can take up to
24 hours to show the first scan.

## Delayed or lost parcels
If tracking hasn't moved for 5 business days, or the parcel is marked delivered but you
can't find it, contact us and we'll open an investigation with the carrier. We'll send a
replacement or refund once the carrier confirms the loss, usually within 7 days.

## Changing the address
We can't change the delivery address once an order is placed. If the order hasn't shipped
yet, our team can cancel it so you can reorder.
