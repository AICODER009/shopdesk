# Payments and invoices

## Accepted payment methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna (pay in 30 days)
and SEPA direct debit for customers in Germany and Austria.

## When you're charged
Card and wallet payments are authorised when you order and captured when the order ships.
If part of an order ships later, you're charged for each part as it ships.

## Payment declined
Declines come from your bank or card issuer, not from us. Check the card's expiry date and
billing address, or try another method. We never ask for your full card number or PIN by
chat, email or phone.

## Invoices
A VAT invoice is attached to the shipping confirmation email. You can also download it
from **Orders** in your account. We can't change the name or address on an invoice after
it's issued.

## Unrecognised charges
If you see a charge from Northwind you don't recognise, contact us before raising a dispute
with your bank. Our team will check it with you.
