# Your account and your data

## Updating your details
Change your name, email, password and saved addresses under **Account > Settings**. For
security we can't change the email address on an account for you over chat.

## Loyalty tiers
Silver starts at €250 spent in 12 months, Gold at €750, Platinum at €2,000. Tiers are
reviewed monthly. Gold and Platinum get free express shipping in Germany and longer returns.

## Your data rights
You can ask for a copy of your personal data, for corrections, or for your account and data
to be deleted. Our support team handles these requests and confirms your identity first. We
answer within 30 days, as the GDPR requires. Order records we must keep for tax purposes are
retained for 10 years even after account deletion, but are no longer used for marketing.

## Chat assistant and your data
Chat conversations are kept for 30 days and then deleted. You can delete a conversation at
any time from the chat menu. Conversations handed to our team become part of your support
ticket.
