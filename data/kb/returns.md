# Returns and refunds

## Time limits
You can return unworn, unwashed items with their original tags within 30 days of delivery.
Gold and Platinum members get 60 days. Sale items marked "final sale" can't be returned.

## How to start a return
Sign in, open **Orders**, choose the order and select **Return items**. Print the prepaid
label, or show the QR code at any DHL drop-off point. You don't need the original box.

## Refund timing
We refund to the original payment method within 5 business days of the parcel reaching our
warehouse. Your bank may take another 3 to 5 days to show it. Original shipping costs are
refunded only if the whole order is returned because it arrived faulty.

## International returns
Returns from outside the EU are free for orders over €150. Below that, a €9.95 return fee
is deducted from the refund. Customs duties paid on delivery are not refundable by us; ask
your local customs office.

## Faulty or wrong items
If something arrived damaged or isn't what you ordered, don't use the standard return.
Contact us with a photo within 14 days of delivery and we'll send a replacement or a full
refund including shipping.
