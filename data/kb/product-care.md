# Product care

## Merino wool
Wash at 30°C on a wool cycle with wool detergent, or by hand. Dry flat, out of direct
sun. Don't tumble dry. Small pills on the surface are normal in the first weeks and can be
removed with a fabric comb.

## Waxed cotton jackets
Don't machine wash. Sponge off dirt with cold water. Re-wax once a year, or when the jacket
stops beading water, using our re-proofing wax.

## Warranty
All outerwear has a 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Normal
wear, accidents and damage from incorrect care aren't covered.
